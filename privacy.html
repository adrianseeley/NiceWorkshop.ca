Privacy & analytics.

Updated November 1, 2026.

Your cart and orders

Your browser saves your cart on your device so it can be there when you return. When you continue to checkout, we save the requested cuts, prices, fulfillment choice, and any delivery address you provide. Shopify handles checkout and payment. These order functions do not depend on usage analytics.

Usage analytics

This site automatically sends usage analytics to PostHog when analytics is enabled. This helps us understand which shapes, thicknesses, sizes, quantities, and prices people consider, what goes into carts, and where people stop before checkout. We also measure page visits, approximate active time, device category, and the referring website’s domain.

Events use a random visit identifier rather than your name or email. We don’t send delivery addresses, payment details, form contents, URL search parameters, or recordings of your screen. PostHog receives your IP address through the connection and can use it to estimate your country, region, or city. The location can be inaccurate.

A visit identifier is kept for the current browser tab and renewed after 30 minutes without activity. If this browser previously saved a choice declining analytics, we continue to respect it. We also respect Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals. Stopping future reporting does not erase events already sent.

Our PostHog project processes analytics in the United States. We use its free analytics plan, with a one-year event retention period. See PostHog’s privacy policy for information about its processing.

Automated traffic

Separately, when crawler measurement is enabled, we count a limited number of successful homepage requests from recognizable automated crawlers. These records include the crawler’s software name and the public homepage address, with no IP address or search parameters. This is an estimate of crawler requests, not a count of people.
